# Frequently Asked Questions

## What is Late Night Raid?

[Late Night Raid](https://latenightraid.com/) is an independent information hub for World of Warcraft: Forever. It organizes official details into practical guides for returning and curious players.

## Is Late Night Raid an official Blizzard website?

No. Late Night Raid is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Blizzard Entertainment. For official announcements, account help, purchases, and technical support, use Blizzard's official websites and support channels.

## What is this GitHub repository for?

This repository is the project's public documentation home. It contains the project overview, roadmap, changelog, FAQ, and contribution guidance.

## Is the website source code available here?

No. The production website source code, private infrastructure, credentials, and proprietary configuration are not published in this repository.

## Where does the information come from?

Late Night Raid prioritizes first-party sources such as official World of Warcraft product pages, Blizzard news posts, panel recaps, and support articles. Important website guides link to their relevant sources.

## How current is the information?

The website is updated as official information changes or expands. Time-sensitive pages should be checked against their displayed update date and linked sources, especially before a beta, reservation window, or launch event.

## How can I report a correction or broken link?

[Open a GitHub issue](https://github.com/mgayy520/late-night-raid/issues) and include:

- The affected page or section
- The detail that appears incorrect or outdated
- The proposed correction
- A first-party source, when available

Please do not include account details, credentials, or other private information.

## Can I suggest a guide or tool?

Yes. Open an issue describing the player question, who it helps, and why the proposed guide or tool would be useful. See the [roadmap](ROADMAP.md) and [contribution guide](CONTRIBUTING.md) first to avoid duplicate proposals.

## Are roadmap items guaranteed?

No. The roadmap communicates direction and current priorities. It does not guarantee features or release dates.
